How long does tint usually last?

The original treatment guidance states approximately three to four weeks, although this varies by client and aftercare.

Can eyebrows be waxed?

Yes. High-quality Lycon wax is available. Please note this in advance when booking.

Can eyelash and eyebrow tinting be booked together?

Yes. Choose the combined eyelash and eyebrow tint appointment.
