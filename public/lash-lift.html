Helpful to know

Lash-lift questions,
answered simply.

Is lash tint included?

Yes. Lash tint is included with the LVL Enhance lash-lift appointments.

Can eyebrows be included?

Yes. Choose the lash lift and eyebrow appointment when you would like eyebrow tinting included.

Does a lash lift add extensions?

No. A lash lift works with your natural lashes and does not add extensions.
